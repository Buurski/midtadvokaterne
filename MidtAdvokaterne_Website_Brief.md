# MidtAdvokaterne A/S — Full Website Design Brief
### For use when building the new website · Prepared April 2026

---

## 1. Who Is MidtAdvokaterne?

MidtAdvokaterne A/S is a locally rooted Danish law firm headquartered at Sieferts Plads 5, 7430 Ikast — right in the centre of town. They are, by all accounts, the law firm Ikast has relied on since 1964. That is not a marketing claim. That is a fact that sets them apart from every competitor in the region.

The firm was founded by lawyer Ejner Jensen, who opened an office in Ikast in 1964. Since 1981, the firm has operated from the same address on Sieferts Plads — a central, recognisable location that carries genuine meaning for local residents. Over decades, the firm grew from a one-man office into a small but highly experienced team of seven people, anchored by two senior partners who together bring over 30 years of individual legal experience.

Today the firm serves both private individuals and business clients across the Ikast-Herning area and the wider Midtjylland region. They are not a corporate factory. They are a firm built on personal relationships, local knowledge, and a deep understanding of the people and businesses that make up this part of Denmark.

## 4. The Brand Identity

### Core values
- **Rooted** — Over 60 years in the same town. They are part of Ikast's fabric.
- **Personal** — Seven people. You speak to someone who knows your name.
- **Experienced** — Senior partners with decades of real-world practice, not junior associates.
- **Trustworthy** — This is the firm families and businesses come back to, generation after generation.

### Tone of voice
Warm, clear, and calm. No legal jargon on the website. Write in the language a stressed person uses when they Google "advokat skilsmisse Ikast" at 11pm. The copy should never intimidate. It should always reassure. Think: the most knowledgeable friend you have, who also happens to be a lawyer.

### Visual identity direction
The palette should feel rooted in Midtjylland — earthy, grounded, Scandinavian. Suggestions:

- **Primary colour:** Deep navy blue (#1A2B45) or a rich forest green (#1E3A2F) — both signal trust and longevity.
- **Secondary/accent colour:** Warm amber or antique gold (#C4973A) — used sparingly for buttons, hover states, and highlights. This bridges tradition and warmth.
- **Background:** Warm off-white (#F7F4EF) rather than clinical white. Feels like quality paper, like a law office.
- **Text:** Near-black charcoal (#1C1C1C), never pure black.

**Typography:**
- **Headings:** A humanist serif such as *Playfair Display*, *Cormorant Garamond*, or *Libre Baskerville* — signals tradition, authority, permanence.
- **Body text:** A clean, readable sans-serif such as *Inter*, *DM Sans*, or *Nunito* — modern, accessible, fast to read on mobile.
- **Pairing logic:** The serif carries the firm's history; the sans-serif carries its clarity and approachability. Together they say: "We've been doing this for 60 years and we'll explain it to you in plain Danish."

## 5. Page Structure

The website should be clean and deliberate — not a maze of pages. Five pages maximum. Every page should have one clear job.

### Pages:
1. **Forside (Homepage)** — First impression, trust signals, and the team reveal
2. **Vores Hold (Meet the Team)** — Integrated into the homepage scroll; not a separate page
3. **Retsområder (Practice Areas)** — One page with clearly structured service sections
4. **Om Os / Vores Historie (About & History)** — The story of the firm from 1964 to today
5. **Kontakt (Contact)** — Form, map, phone, opening hours, client type split

## 6. Homepage — Detailed Design Vision

The homepage is the most important page. It does everything: it earns trust, introduces the people, signals professionalism, and converts visitors into enquiries. Everything below describes how it should look and feel as you scroll from top to bottom.

---

### Section 1: Hero — Full-Screen Opening

**What the visitor sees first:**

A full-viewport-height hero section. No clutter. No menu taking up half the screen. Just one powerful image and one powerful sentence.

**The hero image:** A high-quality photograph of the exterior of Sieferts Plads 5 — the actual building — shot in soft, golden-hour light. Ideally late afternoon in autumn or spring, when the Midtjylland light is warm and low. The building should feel like an institution, not just an office. Alternatively, a wide interior shot of the reception or meeting room — polished wood, natural light, ordered bookshelves.

**The animation:** The hero image should have a very subtle **Ken Burns effect** — a slow, barely-perceptible zoom-in or pan across the image that takes 12-15 seconds to complete before looping. This is enough movement to feel alive and cinematic without being distracting. Think of the opening shot of a quality documentary film. It breathes.

**Overlay:** A semi-transparent dark overlay (dark navy at ~40% opacity) sits on top of the photo, ensuring the white text is always readable regardless of the image.

**The headline text:**
```
Erfarne advokater i hjertet af Ikast.
Siden 1964.
```
The headline fades in slowly (800ms fade-up animation) after the page loads. The subtitle or supporting line appears 300ms later beneath it:
```
Personlig juridisk rådgivning til private og erhverv — tæt på dig.
```

**CTA buttons:** Two buttons appear below the headline:
- Primary: `Kontakt os` (amber/gold background, navy text)
- Secondary: `Se vores ydelser` (outlined, white border and text)

**Scroll indicator:** A small animated downward arrow or line-pulse at the bottom centre of the hero section, inviting the visitor to scroll. It pulses gently — a subtle cue, not an interruption.

**Navigation bar:** Minimal, transparent over the hero, with the firm name or logo on the left and four navigation links on the right (Retsområder, Om Os, Kontakt, and possibly a language toggle if needed). The nav becomes a solid white/navy bar when the user scrolls past the hero.

---

### Section 2: Trust Bar (Thin, High-Impact)

Immediately below the hero, a thin full-width horizontal strip containing three or four short trust signals in a row. Clean, icon + text format:

```
⚖ Grundlagt 1964     👥 7 dedikerede medarbejdere     📍 Centrum af Ikast     ✅ Erhverv & privat
```

These animate in one by one from left to right as the section enters the viewport — a staggered fade-in with 150ms delays between each item. Simple, effective, instantly communicates key facts.

---

### Section 3: The Firm in Two Sentences

A wide centred text block — almost like a quote — that frames what MidtAdvokaterne is. Large, elegant serif text:

> *"Vi er ikke Danmarks største advokatfirma. Vi er Ikasts."*

Below it, three to four lines in smaller body text explaining in plain language what the firm offers: personal service, local roots, experienced lawyers, real relationships. This section should have generous white space above and below it. Let it breathe. It is a pause before the team reveal.

The text fades up as it enters the viewport. Smooth, 600ms, no bounce.

---

### Section 4: Meet the Team (Scroll-Reveal, Integrated into Homepage)

This is the centrepiece of the homepage and the section you most want to get right. The idea is that as the visitor scrolls down, the team reveals itself — not in a list, but in a way that feels warm, human, and deliberate.

**Layout concept:**

A full-width section with a dark navy or deep off-white background (contrast from the white sections above). The heading at the top reads:

```
Mød holdet
```

Below this, the team members appear in a staggered card layout as the user scrolls. Each card contains:

- A large, high-quality **black-and-white portrait photograph** of the team member (colour photos are also fine — but B&W or duotone gives a cohesive, editorial feel even if different photographers shot each person)
- Their **name** in serif font, large and clear
- Their **title** (Partner, Advokat, Sekretær, etc.)
- Their **two or three key practice areas** listed in small text
- A **direct phone number** — clickable on mobile
- A subtle hover state: on desktop, hovering over the card causes it to lift slightly (translateY -8px) and the photo shifts from B&W to full colour. This is the kind of detail that makes a website feel crafted and alive.

**Animation sequence:**

As the user scrolls into this section, the cards animate in using a staggered reveal:
- The section background fades in first (200ms)
- Then the heading slides up from below (400ms)
- Then each team card fades in and slides up, one by one, left to right, with 120ms stagger between each (so they cascade in, not all at once)

This creates a genuine sense of a curtain being lifted — of people stepping forward to introduce themselves. It should feel natural and smooth, like the pages of a well-designed magazine coming to life.

**Partners first:** The two partners appear in larger cards at the top (perhaps full-width side by side on desktop), followed by the remaining five team members in a three-column grid below. On mobile, all cards stack vertically in a single column.

**Photo style guidance for the actual photoshoot:**
- Natural light, not studio flash
- Taken at or near the Sieferts Plads office
- Relaxed but composed — not stiff headshots, not overly casual
- All shot on the same background (a wall in the office, or a specific outdoor wall of the building) for visual cohesion
- Each person should be looking directly into the camera — connection matters
- Avoid generic handshake/laptop/briefcase poses

### Section 5: Client Split (Privat / Erhverv)

A clean two-column section (side by side on desktop, stacked on mobile), one card for private clients, one for business clients. Each card has:

- A relevant photo (home/family for private; office/building for business)
- A short heading: "Til privatpersoner" / "Til erhvervslivet"
- Three to four bullet practice areas relevant to each
- A CTA button: "Læs mere" linking to the relevant section of the Retsområder page

These two cards slide in from opposite sides (left and right) as the section enters the viewport — another scroll-triggered animation that adds flow without feeling gimmicky.

---

### Section 6: Quote or Testimonial

A full-width section with a warm background — perhaps a lightly textured off-white or a muted sage green. A single client quote, presented large and centred:

> *"MidtAdvokaterne har hjulpet vores familie igennem nogle af de sværeste perioder i vores liv — med ro, kompetence og ægte omsorg."*
> — Privat klient, Ikast

If real client testimonials aren't available at launch, a quote from one of the partners about their philosophy works equally well. This section has no animation — it is intentionally still. A moment of pause.

The phone number should be large, prominent, and click-to-call on mobile. The firm's single biggest conversion tool is the phone call — make it as easy as possible.

Clean, minimal. Three columns:

A thin line at the very bottom with copyright and a note about cookies/privacy policy (required by Danish/EU law).

## 7. Practice Areas Page

A single scrollable page — not a tab system, not a dropdown maze. A clear introduction at the top, then each practice area as its own named section with:

- A small icon or illustration beside the area name
- A two-to-three paragraph plain-language description: "What is it, when do you need it, and how do we help you?"
- The name of the partner who handles this area, with a small photo and link to their profile section
- A CTA: "Har du spørgsmål? Ring til os."

Between sections, use thin dividers and plenty of white space. The page should feel calm and informative, not like a legal document.

## 8. About & History Page

This page tells the story. It should feel like a long-form editorial piece — warm, proud, and human. Not a Wikipedia entry.

**Structure:**

**Opening:** A full-width photo of the Sieferts Plads building today. Below it, the headline:
```
En del af Ikast siden 1964.
```

**The story in four chapters:**
1. **1964 — Grundlæggelsen** — Ejner Jensen opens the office. What Ikast was like then. The beginning of a relationship between a law firm and a community.
2. **1981 — Et nyt hjem** — The move to Sieferts Plads. Still there today. A permanent presence in the town centre.
3. **Vækst og kontinuitet** — How the firm grew. Partners joining. The values that were maintained through every transition.
4. **I dag** — Seven people. Two experienced partners. The same commitment, updated for the modern world.

**Photo timeline:** Between chapters, use archival or present-day photos of the building, the town, the team. If old photos of the original office exist — use them. Nothing builds trust like genuine history.

**Values section:** At the bottom of the page, three or four illustrated values in a grid:
- Nærhed (Closeness — we know your name)
- Erfaring (Experience — 60 years of real cases)
- Ærlighed (Honesty — we tell you what you need to hear)
- Engagement (Commitment — this is our town too)

## 10. Technical & Performance Requirements

- **HTTPS is mandatory** — the current HTTP site is unacceptable in 2026
- **Mobile-first design** — the majority of local searches happen on phones
- **Google Business Profile integration** — embed or link to reviews, ensure the address matches exactly
- **Page load under 3 seconds** — compress all images, use modern formats (WebP), lazy-load below-the-fold images
- **Cookie consent banner** — required under Danish/EU law
- **Privacy policy page** — required, linked in footer
- **Sitemap and robots.txt** — for Google indexing
- **SEO-optimised meta titles and descriptions** per page (e.g., "Advokat i Ikast | MidtAdvokaterne A/S | Familieret, Erhverv og mere")
- **No .aspx extensions** — clean URL structure (e.g., /retsomraader, /om-os, /kontakt)

## 11. Animation Summary — The Rules

The animations on this site should follow one principle: **they add flow, they do not demand attention.**

| What | How | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Hero image | Ken Burns slow zoom/pan | 12–15s loop |
| Hero headline | Fade up on load | 800ms |
| Trust bar items | Staggered fade in, left to right | 150ms delay each |
| Section headings | Slide up on scroll enter | 500ms |
| Team cards | Staggered slide up + fade, left to right | 120ms stagger |
| Team card hover | Lift + B&W to colour photo | 300ms transition |
| Client split cards | Slide in from opposite sides | 600ms |
| Contact form button | Subtle scale on hover | 200ms |
| Nav bar | Transparent → solid on scroll | 300ms |

All animations should respect **prefers-reduced-motion** — if a visitor has that accessibility setting enabled, all animations are disabled and content appears instantly. This is both good practice and required for accessibility compliance.

## 13. The Single Most Important Thing

Everything about this website should answer one unspoken question that every visitor brings with them:

**"Can I trust these people with one of the most difficult things happening in my life right now?"**

The answer is delivered through:
- A real building they can picture
- Real faces they can see
- Real experience they can measure (since 1964)
- Language they can understand
- A phone number they can press

If the website achieves those five things clearly, beautifully, and quickly — it works.
